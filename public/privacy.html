Privacy

How Tre treats your information.

Your data is yours

Everything you enter (income, bills, budgets, expenses, goals, balances) belongs to your household and is stored in this app's private Firebase project. Only the members of your household can read it. It is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Sign in

Sign in is with Google only. The app stores your Google account id, your email, and your first name so your household knows who logged what. No password ever touches the app.

Receipts and screenshots

When you photograph a receipt or import a statement image, the image is sent to an AI service to read the amounts, then discarded. The app keeps only the expense lines you approve. Nothing is logged without your confirmation.

Bank balances (optional)

If you connect a brokerage through Plaid, the connection is read only: the app receives balances, never credentials. The sign in happens inside Plaid's secure window, and the access token is held on the server where no browser can read it. You can disconnect anytime, and entering balances by hand always works.

AI suggestions

The savings suggestions are generated from a snapshot of your own numbers, sent to the AI provider for that one request. The app never lets the model invent a figure: every dollar amount shown is computed from your data.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry in the app at any time. To remove the household entirely, ask the person who runs the Firebase project to delete it; nothing is retained elsewhere.
